IMU-language models trained on 3D motion confuse sitting, standing and lying

SetCon estimates the mounting and keeps the direction of gravity relative to the body, which separates these postures.

56 % higher zero-shot accuracy than the previous state of the art on 13 real datasets

SetCon with anchors reaches 65.3 % against 41.8 % for AnyMo with class names. Anchors average the text embeddings of a class name and of activity descriptions and need no labeled window.

SetCon rows give the mean of three seeds. UniMTS and AnyMo are scored on the same windows as SetCon in the last row.
