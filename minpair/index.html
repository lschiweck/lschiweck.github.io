This work was funded by the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) within the project AIDA (grant FEIH 2476184).

The examples are selected, the numbers cover the full benchmarks of the paper. The bodies are rendered videos. The two clips of the training pair come from AMASS (Mahmood et al., ICCV 2019), originally from the CMU and KIT motion capture databases, every other body is generated by MinPair from a held-out caption. Body model: SMPL-H. Instrument Sans (OFL).
